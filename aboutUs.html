A neighborhood bakery, made for everyday moments.

At BreadTalk, the day begins with the warmth of the oven and the smell of fresh bread. We make familiar favorites with care, pour a proper cup of coffee, and believe the best things taste even better when shared.

Drop by for a quick pastry, linger over breakfast, or take home a loaf for the table. However you visit, there is always room for one more.
